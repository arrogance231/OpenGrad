# Roadmap

OpenGrad proceeds from infrastructure to controlled measurement. A later phase is not considered successful without reproducible evidence and regression analysis.

0. Repository and research infrastructure
0.5. CPU-only pre-experiment validation
1. Baseline reproduction
2. Tool-calling dataset preparation and audit
3. First controlled SFT experiment
4. Full evaluation and diagnosis
5. Preference optimization only if justified
6. On-policy distillation
7. Cross-model replication
8. Quantization and runtime evaluation
9. OpenWeights on-device studies
10. Speculative decoding and inference research
11. Joint capability–efficiency optimization
